For animal lovers looking to adopt, foster, or take care of pets, and for animal protection associations struggling with administrative overload, _Patas_ is a collaborative web platform that centralizes and simplifies the entire animal care and adoption cycle.

Unlike manual processes relying on spreadsheets, chaotic group chats, and scattered applications that leave adopters in the dark, our product provides a streamlined, stress-free solution. _Patas_ combines an open exploration route with smart lifestyle matching, full transparency through a real-time Application Tracker, and a guided Foster-to-Adopt model. By doing so, we maximize compatibility between families and pets, drastically reduce the risk of returns, and relieve associations of heavy manual workloads, our main priority is providing these animals with love and a better life

Options:
- Foster: someone volunteers to provide housing and care to one or multiple animals for a period of time.
- Adopt: people can look for the animal that better fits their home and family and adopt them, a temporary hosting is possible before finalizing the decision, ensuring that the fit is right.
- Care: users can volunteer to walk a certain number of animals, feed them, clean them or take them to the vet.
