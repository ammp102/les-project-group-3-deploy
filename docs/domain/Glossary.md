# Domain Glossary (Ubiquitous Language)

This document defines the core terminology for the **Patas** platform. Its purpose is to establish a Ubiquitous Language to be shared across the development team, domain experts, and stakeholders, ensuring that code and communication use the exact same vocabulary. Lifecycles are in [StateMachines](StateMachines.md); the object model is in [DomainModel](DomainModel.md).

## 1. Actors & Roles
*   **Visitor:** An unauthenticated user browsing the public catalog, viewing animal profiles and wishlists, or taking the Matchmaker quiz. Visitors cannot apply, pledge or sign up for anything.
*   **Citizen:** A registered, authenticated user.
*   **Volunteer:** A Citizen who has signed up to offer their time for walks, socialization, or operational shifts at an Association.
*   **Foster Family:** A Citizen who has activated the fostering role and registered their home conditions, capacity and availability to provide temporary housing for animals.
*   **Association Staff:** A user linked to a specific Association, responsible for managing its animals, applications, rosters, wishlists and trial-fee settings. Staff only see their own Association's data.
*   **Platform Administrator:** A super-user who manages the platform globally, verifying new Associations and moderating content.

## 2. Core Entities & Concepts
*   **Association:** An animal protection organization or shelter that registers on the platform to manage its animals, operations, and adoption processes. It must be verified before publishing animals.
*   **Verification Status:** The state of an Association's registration: *Pending*, *Verified*, *Rejected* or *Suspended*.
*   **Animal:** A pet (e.g., dog, cat) under the care of an Association, registered in the system with its profile (age, size, temperament, compatibility), needs, and medical history.
*   **Adoption Application (Application):** The formal process and documentation submitted by a Citizen to adopt a specific Animal.
*   **Adoption Tracker:** The system module that provides real-time, transparent tracking of an Application's status (timeline, built from the status history) to the Citizen.
*   **Notification:** A message generated by a state change, delivered both by email and in-app (notification center).
*   **Application Kanban:** The back-office board where Association Staff move Applications between status columns and add private Screening Notes.
*   **Screening Note:** A private note added by Association Staff to an Application; never visible to the applicant.
*   **Matchmaker:** The optional compatibility system that suggests Animals to Visitors/Citizens based on a lifestyle and routine questionnaire.
*   **Matchmaker Profile:** The saved, editable lifestyle answers of a Citizen (activity level, housing type, time away from home, previous experience). Visitors' answers are not saved.
*   **Foster-to-Adopt (Trial Period):** An optional phase within the Adoption Application where the Citizen takes the Animal home for a short, agreed-upon period to ensure adaptation before finalizing the legal adoption. The adopter requests it; the Association approves or declines it.
*   **Trial Status:** The lifecycle of a Trial Period: *Requested*, *Approved*, *Active*, *Succeeded*, *Failed*, *Declined*, *Cancelled*.
*   **Trial Fee:** An optional amount the Association charges for a Trial Period, paid online through the platform (Stripe, test mode).
*   **Check-in:** A dated note logged by Association Staff during an active Trial Period to follow the animal's adaptation.
*   **Temporary Fostering:** The act of a Foster Family temporarily housing an Animal (independent of an adoption process). Not to be confused with Foster-to-Adopt.
*   **Housing Conditions:** The physical characteristics of a Foster Family's home (e.g., house with garden, fenced yard, apartment with safety netting, play space).
*   **Parametric Fostering Engine:** The search tool where Staff filter Foster Families by matching an Animal's needs against the families' parameters (species, size, conditions, capacity, time window).
*   **Foster Assignment:** The record linking an Animal to a Foster Family. It starts as a proposal, must be accepted by the family, and drives the Animal's Location.
*   **Operational Roster:** A schedule of shifts managed by an Association for core facility tasks (e.g., feeding, cleaning) that Volunteers can sign up for.
*   **Walk / Socialization Session:** Specific time slots available for Volunteers to spend time with Animals to improve their well-being.
*   **Wishlist:** A reverse marketplace list published by an Association detailing specific in-kind items (goods) they currently need.
*   **Pledge:** A commitment made by a Citizen to provide a specific quantity of an item from an Association's Wishlist. The item remains "pledged" until the Association confirms its physical receipt.
*   **Target Quantity:** A soft target set by Staff for a Wishlist item. It is not a limit: donors can pledge any quantity while the item is listed.
*   **Coverage:** For a Wishlist item, the confirmed plus pledged quantity divided by the target quantity.
*   **Priority Level:** *High*, *Medium* or *Low*, derived from Coverage and used to order the Wishlist (High first). It never blocks a pledge.
*   **Closed Item:** A Wishlist item the Association removed from the public list because it has enough. It takes no new pledges; pledges already made can still be confirmed or released.

## 3. Animal Lifecycle & States
*   **Adoption Status:** The state dimension representing the animal's progress towards a permanent home.
    *   *Available:* Ready to receive new Adoption Applications.
    *   *Reserved:* One application has reached Interview; other open applications are put On Hold.
    *   *In Trial:* Currently in an active Foster-to-Adopt trial period.
    *   *Adopted:* The adoption process is finalized. If the owner later returns the animal, it becomes Available again.
    *   *Unavailable:* Not ready for adoption (e.g., undergoing medical treatment, behavioral issues).
*   **Location:** The physical whereabouts of the animal (independent of adoption status).
    *   *At Shelter:* Physically located at the Association's facilities.
    *   *Awaiting Pickup:* A Foster Family has accepted the assignment, but the animal is still at the shelter waiting to be collected.
    *   *In Foster Home:* Physically located at a temporary Foster Family's residence.
    *   *With Adopter:* Physically with the adopter, during a trial period and after adoption.

## 4. Application Status (States)
*   **Submitted:** Application received by the Association; awaiting review.
*   **Screening:** Association Staff is actively reviewing the application details.
*   **On Hold:** The animal is reserved for another application; this application is paused and resumes if the reservation is released.
*   **Interview:** Association Staff is in direct communication with the applicant. Entering this state reserves the Animal.
*   **Trial Period:** The Animal is currently with the applicant for an active Foster-to-Adopt trial.
*   **Completed:** The adoption is finalized and successful.
*   **Rejected:** The application was declined by the Association.
*   **Withdrawn:** The application was canceled by the Citizen.
*   **Closed:** The application was closed because the Animal was adopted by someone else.

## 5. Foster Assignment Status (States)
*   **Proposed:** Staff selected a Foster Family; awaiting the family's answer.
*   **Accepted:** The family accepted; the animal's location becomes Awaiting Pickup.
*   **Active:** Staff confirmed pickup; the animal is In Foster Home.
*   **Completed:** The animal returned to the shelter or was handed to an adopter.
*   **Declined:** The family declined the proposal.
*   **Cancelled:** The proposal was withdrawn, or the family cancelled or did not show up.

## 6. Pledge Status (States)
*   **Pledged:** The Citizen committed; awaiting delivery.
*   **Confirmed:** The Association confirmed physical receipt.
*   **Released:** The Association released an unfulfilled pledge, removing its quantity from the item's Coverage.

## 7. Wishlist Item Status (States)
*   **Active:** Listed publicly and accepting pledges, with no upper limit.
*   **Closed:** Removed from the public list by Staff; takes no new pledges. Can be reopened.
